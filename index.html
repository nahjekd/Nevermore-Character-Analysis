Nevermore Character Analysis

What is Nevermore?

Nevermore is a Sapphic Webtoon following Lenore Vandernacht as she guides through the in-between of death and life. Lenore and Annabel Lee find themselves in a mysterious location, where they meet each other without knowledge of their past lives. Together, they enter Nevermore Academy and begin their journey of self-discovery while planning their escape. However, their differing views on who to trust and whether to include their friends complicates their plans. Can they make it out alive, or will the forces around them control their fate?
